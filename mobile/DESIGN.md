# Haddak design system

The visual rules for the Haddak app, as built. The code mirror of this file is `src/theme/tokens.ts`: change both together. Screens and components use tokens only, never a raw colour or size.

Status: rebuilt on 2026-11-16 after the founder rejected the earlier teal design; refined and re-coloured several times on 2026-11-17. **Re-themed on 2026-11-17 as "Pastel"** (founder approval, from his reference: a soft, modern pastel dashboard): ghost-white ground, white and alice-blue blocks, vanilla as the signature highlight, eerie black for actions and the selected state, Urbanist for Latin text. Same day, the founder removed honeydew: **no green anywhere** except WhatsApp's own button. This replaces the purple and tangerine palette everywhere.

## Direction: Pastel

A ghost-white page with white cards and pastel blocks on it, black for everything you press or have chosen. Very round, mostly flat: colour blocks do the separating, shadows are barely there.

- **Ghost white** (`background`) is the page. **White** (`surface`) is a plain card. **Alice blue** (`accent`, `hero`) is the main tinted block: the Home hero, the List / Map track, info panels, the sponsored card, done notices, a verified profile's hero. **Vanilla** (`signature`) is the highlight: Jarak AI, Sponsored, offers, "Available now". **Eerie black** (`primary`) is filled buttons, the selected chip or segment, round arrow buttons and the Verified pill.
- **Chips:** outlined when not chosen (1 dp `border` edge, no fill), solid black with white words when chosen. Distance, filters, the hours and day choices.
- **Round black arrow buttons** (black disc, white arrow): the Ask entry's send, the corner of every category tile, "Everyone nearby", the assistant's summary row.
- **The surface rule.** On the ground: white cards (`elevation.card`, now very soft) and pastel blocks (no shadow). Inside a white card: a faint `surfaceInset` (ghost white) inset, or a pastel tile; a white tile inside a white card gets a `border` edge instead of a fill. Never a grey block inside a grey block.
- **Feel:** calm, light, crafted; clean and airy, like a well-made dashboard.
- **Scene:** a person on an Android phone in daylight, in the street or at home, who needs someone now. Light theme with strong contrast.
- **Signature:** vanilla for Haddak's special moments, and the small black Verified pill for trust.
- **Refused:** green tints (honeydew, mint), purple, tangerine, gradients, glass, heavy shadows, coloured side stripes, white text on pastel, pastel as a text or line colour, a list-of-rows look.

Rules:

- **No green** except WhatsApp. "Open now" is a small solid black dot and the words; success states and confirmed hires are alice blue; Verified is the black pill.
- **Black is for pressing and choosing.** Filled buttons, the chosen chip or segment, round arrow buttons. Unchosen chips are outlined.
- **Vanilla marks Haddak's special moments** (Jarak AI, Sponsored, offers, Available now) and is always a fill with black words. As a thin line it is `signatureStrong`.
- **Pastels are fills only.** Text on alice blue and vanilla is always black (`onAccent`, `onSignature`), never white, and a pastel is never a text colour.
- Contrast (WCAG AA, checked 2026-11-17): `text` 12:1 or more on every fill (ghost white 14.8, alice blue 12.0, vanilla 13.5); `textMuted` 5.4:1 and `textSubtle` 5.0:1 or more on alice blue and every grey, 6.1:1 and 5.6:1 on vanilla; white on `primary` 16:1 (11:1 pressed); `signatureStrong` 3.9:1 on white, 3.5:1 on the ground, 3.2:1 on vanilla; `outline` 3.4:1 on white, 3.1:1 on the ground. The story ring is never the only sign of an update (the stories row says "time ago" in words).
- One elevation system (`elevation` in `tokens.ts`, drawn with `boxShadow`): `card` is a barely-there `0 1px 2px rgba(33,33,33,.03), 0 6px 20px rgba(33,33,33,.04)` on white cards on the ground; `float` (`0 8px 24px rgba(33,33,33,.16)`) only for the card docked over the map. Pastel blocks and anything inside a card have none.

## Type

Latin text is **Urbanist** (Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700, from `@expo-google-fonts/urbanist`, loaded in `src/app/_layout.tsx`). Urbanist has no Arabic letters, so Arabic keeps **Beiruti** (titles) and **Readex Pro** (text and controls). `Txt` picks the set: the Arabic set when the app is in Arabic or the words themselves are Arabic (a provider's Arabic name, the language pill), Urbanist otherwise; text fields use `useTypeScale()` from `Txt.tsx`. Sizes scale with the system font size (`allowFontScaling` stays on).

| Role | Latin (`type`) | Arabic (`typeArabic`) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `display` | Urbanist Medium 32 / 38, -0.8 | Beiruti Bold 30 / 38 | The question on Home, a provider's name, intro blocks, stat figures |
| `poster` | Urbanist SemiBold 40 / 46, -1 | Beiruti Bold 38 / 48 | A short written update, the offer's discount figure |
| `headline` | Urbanist SemiBold 22 / 28, -0.4 | Beiruti Bold 22 / 30 | The wordmark, screen-bar titles, section titles |
| `title` | Urbanist SemiBold 18 / 24, -0.2 | Beiruti SemiBold 18 / 26 | Card names, titles inside a card, fact values |
| `body` / `bodyStrong` | Urbanist 400 / 600, 15 / 22 | Readex Pro 400 / 500, 15 / 24 | Reading text |
| `action` | Urbanist 600, 15 / 22 | Readex Pro 600, 15 / 22 | Buttons, category tiles |
| `data` | Urbanist 600, 14 / 20 | Readex Pro 500, 14 / 20 | Rating, distance, price |
| `label` | Urbanist 500, 13 / 18 | Readex Pro 500, 13 / 20 | Supporting lines, chips, segments |
| `caption` | Urbanist 500, 12 / 16 | Readex Pro 400, 12 / 18 | Dates, small notes |
| `badge` | Urbanist 600, 12 / 16 | Readex Pro 500, 12 / 18 | Words in a badge pill, map pin names |
| `bodyLarge` / `bodyLargeStrong` | Urbanist 400 / 600, 17 / 24 | Readex Pro 400 / 500, 17 / 26 | The assistant's reading text, card lines and facts |
| `actionLarge` | Urbanist 600, 17 / 24 | Readex Pro 600, 17 / 24 | Large buttons |
| `input` | Urbanist 400, 20 / 28 | Readex Pro 400, 20 / 30 | What the user types to the assistant |

Titles are large and light-to-medium, set slightly tight (negative letter-spacing in Latin only; Arabic is never letter-spaced). The assistant is a step bigger on purpose: it is made for people who rarely use a phone. One `display` per screen at most; every screen bar uses `headline`. Section titles get more air above than below (Home: 28 above, 12 below).

## Shape and space

- Radius: `lg` 24 for cards, tiles and white sections; `sheet` 28 for the Home hero, big pastel blocks and the top corners of the profile sheet and bottom sheets; `md` 16 for thumbnails, icon wells, stat tiles and insets inside a card; `sm` 10 for small marks (the Jarak AI mark); `pill` for buttons, fields, chips and segmented controls. An inner radius is always smaller than the card around it.
- Spacing: a 4-point scale on an 8-point rhythm. 16 at the page edge, 12 between cards and tiles, 12 to 16 inside a card, 20 inside the hero and big blocks. Clean and airy.
- Icons: Lucide line icons, 1.75 stroke (`size.stroke`), 2 (`lineBold`) at 16 dp. 16 dp in pills, fact rows and arrow discs, 20 dp in buttons, 24 dp in icon buttons and tile wells.
- Touch targets are 48 dp. Compact controls are 36 to 40 dp to the eye and reach 48 with `hitSlop`. The assistant's main actions are 56 dp (`size.buttonLarge`); the Home entry is 72 dp (`size.askEntry`). Round arrow discs: 28 dp (`size.arrowDisc`) as a mark inside a larger target, 48 to 56 dp when they are the button.
- Motion only marks a state change (120 to 200 ms). No entrance animations.
- Press feedback: buttons, cards, tiles and the Ask entry shrink to 98 % (`press.scale`) while held, and white cards turn `surfaceInset`; Android keeps its ripple. Skeletons pulse gently and stay still under Reduce Motion.

## Components

- **Button** (`Button.tsx`): one pill, 52 dp, shrinks to 98 % while pressed. `primary` black with white words, `whatsapp` green, `tonal` grey, `outline` (a 1 dp `border` edge, no fill), `white` (a quiet action on the ground or a pastel block), `danger` (emergency numbers only). `compact` tightens the padding for the three-button action bar. `large`: 56 dp, `actionLarge` words, a label that wraps to two lines instead of being cut.
- **Distance control** (`RadiusChips.tsx`): four pill chips in a row, 36 dp high: outlined when not chosen, solid black with white words when chosen. Used in one row on Home and in the Results header.
- **Verified filter** (`VerifiedFilter.tsx`): a pill whose name never changes, "Verified only", with a small switch at its end. Off (the default): an outlined pill, mid-grey track, white thumb at the start. On: a solid black pill with white words, white track, black thumb at the end with a white check. `accessibilityRole="switch"`.
- **List / Map switch** (in `results.tsx`): an alice-blue segmented track with two labelled segments (icon and word), the chosen one a black pill with white words.
- **Category tile** (`CategoryTile.tsx`): a white card, 104 dp high, with `elevation.card`: the 24 dp black icon in a 44 dp well (`radius.md`) at the top, the wells alternating like a chessboard between alice blue (tone `alice`) and vanilla (tone `vanilla`), the name at the bottom in `action`, and a 28 dp round black disc with a white 16 dp arrow in the corner (`size.arrowDisc`; chevron opens more choices, diagonal arrow goes to results). Every tile is white; only the wells carry colour. The grid alternates wide + narrow rows with 12 dp gaps.
- **Badges** (`Badges.tsx`): pills, 24 dp, words in the `badge` role. Verified (solid black, white check badge and white words), Not verified (outlined grey, empty badge, muted words), Featured (alice blue, means paid), Sponsored (vanilla, black words), Hired through Haddak (alice blue, handshake), and two quiet marks with no pill around them: Founding member (a medal and the words in `textMuted`) and "Listed by the community".
- **Open status** (`OpenStatus.tsx`): no pill. A small solid black dot plus "Open now", or an empty grey ring plus "Closed". Words are always ink. No green.
- **Provider card** (`ProviderCard.tsx`): a white card with `elevation.card` and 12 dp padding: photo, name, "category · area", the trust line, then a `surfaceInset` strip with rating, distance and price in fixed columns. Pressed: the card turns `surfaceInset` and shrinks to 98 %. **Sponsored** variant (`sponsored: true`): the block is alice blue (its facts strip white), with a vanilla "Sponsored" pill and the sponsor's one-line hook; the API returns it first, so it sits at the top of the list. A running offer is a vanilla pill with a tag icon.
- **Map pin** (`MapParts.tsx`): a pill carrying the provider's name (cut at 18 characters) with a pointer under it. Verified: white pill, black edge, a black disc with a white check. Not verified: grey pill, grey edge, empty ring, muted name. Featured: alice-blue pill. Sponsored: a vanilla disc with a dark edge (or an olive `signatureStrong` ring around the black check when verified). Selected: black pill, larger. When names would overlap, only the most important keep their pill (selected, then sponsored and featured, then verified, then by rank; `layoutPins`); the others become a 22 dp dot (black disc with a white check, grey ring when not verified, alice blue when featured, vanilla when sponsored) drawn under the names. Zooming in or tapping a dot brings its name back. The search position is an alice-blue dot with an ink ring; the search area is a slate-edged circle.
- **Story ring** (`StoryRing.tsx`): the mark for "this provider posted an update": a 2.5 dp olive-yellow ring (`signatureStrong`, 3:1 or more on white, the ground and vanilla) around the photo with a 3 dp gap, keeping the photo's rounded-square shape (never a circle). Ink on an alice-blue block. Used on Home's "Updates nearby" row (`StoriesRow.tsx`: 68 dp ringed photo or initial, name, time ago), on result cards and in the profile hero.
- **Story viewer** (`app/stories/[providerId].tsx`): full-screen modal on the page ground. Opened from a stories row it goes on to the next provider of that row after the last update, and back to the previous one from the first; opened from a profile or a card it plays one provider. Progress segments (grey track, `primary` fill), photo + name + "time ago · category", close. A written update is one alice-blue (`hero`) block with black words: category icon on a soft white (`heroControl`) square at the top, the words at the bottom in `poster` / `display` / `headline` by length. With a photo, the photo fills the block and the words sit on a white block over its lower edge. Under it: a black "View profile" pill and "Hold to pause". Six seconds each; no auto-advance under reduced motion or a screen reader.
- **Offer** (`OfferBlock.tsx`, `OfferTag` in `Badges.tsx`): on a profile, a vanilla block with black words (title, one sentence, "Ends in N days") and the discount as a black `poster` figure on a white tile (a tag icon on a small white square when there is no figure). On a card, a vanilla pill with a tag icon and the title.
- **Sponsored section** (in `results.tsx`): at most two sponsored cards under a "Sponsored / Paid placement" heading, then "All results"; category and everyone searches only, list view only. A sponsored provider outside the section is drawn as a plain card.
- **Follow** (profile): follower count on the start side, a 40 dp black "Follow" pill at the end (white with a check once following). Signed out it calls `requireSignIn()` (`src/lib/auth.ts`), which opens sign-in and follows when it closes. Signed in, the pill and count change at once and go back with a notice if the request fails.
- **Form parts** (`Field.tsx`): the Suggest field pattern shared by sign-in and My business (label set in, grey pill field, black edge on focus, red on error, hint or error under it) and `Notice`: a block for one message, red-soft (error), white (information) or alice blue (done).
- **Code boxes** (sign-in): six white 60 dp tiles on the ground over one invisible field, so typing, pasting and the keyboard's code suggestion work; the next box to fill has the `primary` edge. Digits run left to right in Arabic too.
- **Sheet** (`Sheet.tsx`): one small task over a dimmed screen (`scrim`): the page ground with `sheet` top corners, a grab handle, a `headline` title and a grey round close. Back, the X and a tap on the dimmed part close it. Fades in.
- **Reviews card** (`ProviderReviews.tsx`): a white card with `elevation.card`: the average as a `display` figure beside its stars and count; my own review in a `surfaceInset` inset with tonal Edit and Delete (Delete asks once, in place); otherwise a `primary` "Write a review" pill. Then the latest three, and a white pill "See all N reviews" that opens the rest a page at a time.
- **Review sheet** (`ReviewSheet.tsx`): five 32 dp stars in 48 dp targets on a white block (a radio group, the word for the choice under them: Poor to Excellent), the name shown with the review, an optional comment.
- **Report** (`ReportSheet.tsx`): a quiet underlined "Report this listing" line with a flag at the very end of a profile. The sheet lists the reasons as white pills (the chosen one black, with a white check disc), optional details, "We don't save anything about you", then a white thank-you block with an alice-blue check disc.
- **Available now** (`AvailableTag` in `Badges.tsx`): a vanilla pill with a filled black lightning icon and "Available now" in black, on cards, the assistant cards and the profile's badge row. It replaces the open / closed status on a card while it is on. Lightning and vanilla, not a check and black, so it never reads as Verified.
- **Available now switch** (`AvailableNowSection.tsx`, My business): a white block with the title (and the tag while on), the `SwitchRow` (hint "On until 3:30 PM" or "Off"), then "For how long" with three equal outlined pill chips, 2, 4 and 8 hours, the chosen one solid black. Picking another length while on restarts the count. An alice-blue notice confirms.
- **Poster** (`app/poster/[slug].tsx`): two segments on an alice-blue track, "Your business" and "For a building", the chosen one black. The paper is `qrPaper` with `qrInk` only, at least A4 height (`size.posterRatio`), with the QR code at `size.posterQrShare` of its width. English and Arabic sit side by side in two columns split by a hairline, always English left and Arabic right (printed, never mirrored); the two names at the two top corners; thick ink rules above and below the middle. Business: name (`poster`), category, the code of the profile link, "Scan to call or WhatsApp", the link, then "Find trusted help near you: Haddak". Building: the question for the natour's residents, the code of `https://haddak.app`, and a dashed empty "Natour code" box. Under the paper: Share link, Print (web only) and a one-line print hint.
- **Share sticker** (`ShareProfile.tsx`, props `slug` and `name`): the name, a QR code of `https://haddak.app/p/<slug>` and the Haddak wordmark in `qrInk` on `qrPaper` (pure white in both themes, with a 16 dp quiet zone) so it prints and scans as a door sticker; a `primary` "Share link" pill under it, and a tonal "Print a poster" pill when the caller passes `onPoster`. The profile opens it in a sheet from the share button.
- **Switch row** (`SwitchRow.tsx`): an on/off setting in a form. A `surfaceInset` block (`radius.md`) with the label and a one-line hint at the start and a small switch at the end: off is a mid-grey track with an empty thumb at the start, on is a black track with a white thumb and check at the end. The block stays grey. `accessibilityRole="switch"`.
- **Business form** (`ProviderForm.tsx`, shared by List your business and Edit listing): white blocks (`radius.lg`, `elevation.card`) on the ground, one per topic (What you do, How people reach you, Where you are, Opening hours, Photos, Proof), each with a `headline` title and an optional note; grey pill fields inside (`Field.tsx`); the `primary` button and any error docked above the keyboard; a focused field scrolls itself clear of the keyboard. Parts:
  - `CategoryPicker.tsx`: the Suggest category pill and its grey list with a white search pill.
  - `PinPicker.tsx` (`.web.tsx` stand-in): a 240 dp map (`size.mapPicker`) in a `lg` block with one draggable black (`primary`) pin; tapping the map moves it. A tonal "Use my location" button under it.
  - `HoursEditor.tsx`: one row per day (name, Open / Closed, the small switch), then two centred 24-hour time pills (`size.timeField`) when open; "Use Monday's times for every open day" as a text link.
  - `PhotoPicker.tsx`: 96 dp rounded photo tiles (`size.photoTile`, `radius.md`) with a 36 dp scrim remove button at the top end (48 with `hitSlop`), a scrim "Cover" tag on the first, a spinner or "Try again" veil while uploading or after a failure, then a grey "Add photo" tile. The proof is one grey block with an alice-blue icon square that opens the picker; once chosen, the thumbnail with Change and Remove, and a lock line saying only Haddak sees it.
- **Claim card** (profile, listings with no owner): a vanilla block without shadow, a 28 dp white square with a black `Store` icon, the title "Is this your business?", one muted line, a compact black "Claim it for free" button and a quiet "Listed without asking you? The claim sheet (`ClaimSheet.tsx`) is a normal sheet: optional message, optional private proof (`ProofPicker`), Send; its thank-you disc is vanilla.
- **Not verified divider** (Results list): above the first unverified card, a hairline and one line: a `Badge` outline icon, "Not verified yet" in ink, "Haddak hasn't checked these" muted. No box, no colour.
- **OpenStreetMap attribution** (profile, `data_source: "osm"`): "Data © OpenStreetMap contributors" as a muted caption under "Report this listing", linking to the OSM copyright page. With no phone number, WhatsApp and Call are left out of the dock and one muted line says so; Directions stays.
- **Jarak AI mark** (`JarakMark.tsx`): a 28 dp vanilla square (`size.jarakMark`, `radius.sm`) with a 16 dp black `Sparkles` icon, beside the assistant's name on the Home entry and in the assistant's screen bar. Decorative; screen readers skip it.
- **Search bar** (Home, inside the alice-blue block, right under the question; 2026-11-24): a white pill, 60 dp, with a muted magnifier at the start, the words in `bodyLarge`, a clear X once something is typed and a 44 dp round black go button (white arrow) at the end; the edge turns black on focus. - **Cluster pin** (`ClusterPin` in `MapParts.tsx`): pins closer than about 44 dp join one round count (supercluster, plain JavaScript): a white disc with a 2 dp ink edge and the count in black `data` type, inside an alice-blue ring; 40, 48 or 56 dp by count. - **Assistant card** (`AssistCard.tsx`): one of the assistant's top three, compact so two whole cards fit on a 390 x 844 screen under the question (about 222 dp each). - **State view** (`StateView.tsx`): a white 88 dp disc with `elevation.card`, the black icon and a small vanilla dot; errors use the error colours and no shadow.
- **Skeletons** (`Skeleton.tsx`): `surfaceTint` shapes that pulse. On Results they sit inside a white card shaped like the real one (photo, two pill lines, a Verified-sized pill, the inset facts strip), so nothing jumps when the list lands.

## Screens
