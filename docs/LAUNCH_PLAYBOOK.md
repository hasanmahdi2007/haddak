# Haddak launch playbook: getting people in early

Written 2026-11-17. Companion to `IDEAS.md` (research) and `PROJECT.md` (validation rules). Two sides must grow together: **providers** (supply) and **people searching** (demand). Supply comes first, in one small area.

## A. Providers (supply)

| # | Tactic | Why it works in Lebanon | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **List them yourself, on the spot** (3 minutes with their phone: photos, pin, price) | Removes all effort; most owners will never fill a form alone | Time |
| 2 | **Founding member badge + free featured year** for the first 100–200 | Status and a real head start over competitors who join later | $0 |
| 3 | **Free QR door poster** printed for them (Share → Poster) | A visible, physical sign; customers scan instead of asking for the number | ~$0.30 each |
| 4 | **"Your shop's page" link** they can put in their WhatsApp status and Instagram bio | A free mini-website; many shops have none | $0 |
| 5 | **Show them the numbers** (views, WhatsApp taps, confirmed hires) after 1–2 weeks | Proof beats promises; it is what later sells featured placement | $0 |
| 6 | **"Bring three, get featured"**: a provider who brings 3 others gets 3 months featured | Providers know other providers; spreads within trades | $0 |
| 7 | **Start with one trade group**: solar, batteries, generators (power first) | Urgent, high-value, everyone needs them; one clear message | $0 |
| 8 | **Verified badge as a selling point** ("customers trust verified people more") | Trust is the biggest problem in Lebanese services | Your time |
| 9 | **Student area captains** (paid per verified listing, e.g. $1–2) once you stall | Scales your door-to-door without hiring | Small |
| 10 | **Offers / discount banner for free** during launch | Gives shops a reason to post and come back | $0 |

Script for a shop owner: *"I'm launching Haddak, an app where people nearby find you and message you on WhatsApp. Listing is free forever, and the first 100 get a Founding member badge and a free featured year. I'll set it up for you right now, 3 minutes, and print you a QR sign for the door."*

## B. People searching (demand)

| # | Tactic | Why it works in Lebanon | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **WhatsApp number "Ask Haddak"**: people send a voice note or text, you answer with 2–3 providers and the app link | Everyone is on WhatsApp, including older people; no install needed to try | $0 |
| 2 | **Building QR signs with the natour** (elevator or entrance: "Need a plumber, electrician, solar? Scan") | Right where the need happens; neighbours trust each other | ~$0.30 per sign |
| 3 | **Local WhatsApp and Facebook groups** (neighbourhood, university, parents' groups): answer "who knows a good…?" questions with a Haddak link | These questions are asked every day; be the helpful answer, not spam | $0 |
| 4 | **Providers' own customers**: the QR on the door and the link in their status bring their customers into the app | Every provider becomes a distribution channel | $0 |
| 5 | **Seasonal pushes**: before winter (batteries, generators, heaters), before summer (AC service, water tanks), during outages | Ties Haddak to real moments of need | $0 |
| 6 | **Diaspora angle**: "find a trusted technician for your parents in Beirut", shared in diaspora groups | Lebanese abroad pay and worry about family at home | $0 |
| 7 | **Jarak AI as the hook**: short videos of someone typing *"baddé sankari 2arib"* and getting a plumber in 2 seconds | Fun, local, shows the app understands Lebanese | $0 |
| 8 | **University launch**: students as first users (tutors, laptop repair, phone repair, barbers) and as captains | Dense, connected, tech-friendly, you are already there | $0 |
| 9 | **"Did you hire them?" reviews** shown as "Hired through Haddak" | Real, believable reviews make people come back and recommend | Built |
| 10 | **Referral later**: "invite a neighbour, both get a free featured week" (for providers) or early-access perks | Only after there is something worth inviting to | $0 |

## C. Trust signals that make people try a new app

- Verified badge, real photos, real prices and hours on every listing.
- "Confirmed hires" count and "Hired through Haddak" reviews.
- No sign-up needed to search and contact.
- Arabic first, with Lebanese wording; Jarak AI understands Arabizi.
- Free for people searching, always.

## D. What not to do yet

- Paid ads before 50+ providers in one area (people arrive, find nothing, never return).
- Launching in many areas at once.
- Copying listings from Google (against their terms, and those people did not agree).
- Charging providers before you can show them real numbers.
